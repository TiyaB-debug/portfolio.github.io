CityWatch

A team datathon project connecting NYC crash and weather data with tools for public warnings and inspection planning

My contribution
Inspection allocation and optimization
Tools and data
Python, NYC crash data, NOAA weather data
Timeline
November 2026

From risk to a plan

Knowing where risk is higher is only part of the problem. Inspection teams also need a practical way to decide where to go when time and capacity are limited. CityWatch connects crash and weather data with an allocation tool for planning that work.

The part I built

My contribution was the optimization tool. It takes a planning date, the number of teams, and their capacity, then produces inspection-area assignments with a team and visit order. The output also summarizes travel distance and how closely grouped the assigned areas are.

How the pieces fit together

Teammates worked on the predictive model and dashboard. Together, the project brought risk predictions into a satellite map with a public-facing view and an agency view for allocating inspection teams. Assignments could be exported as a CSV.

Working with city data

The project used NYC crash records and NOAA daily weather observations from Central Park, LaGuardia, and JFK. The allocation work turned the project’s risk information into an operational starting point for distributing teams.
