Pandemic response analysis

An analysis of lockdown policies across Brazil, India, and the United States during the COVID-19 pandemic.

The problem

Pandemic policies varied across states and countries. This project examines lockdown-policy effectiveness across different regional contexts.

My contribution

I analyzed global policy data from the Oxford Government Response Tracker using Python to investigate relationships between policy decisions and outcomes.

The approach

I cleaned and transformed the datasets, visualized them using Python and Tableau, and applied regression analysis to evaluate policy effectiveness.

Where it stands

The GitHub repository contains the research materials and analysis.

What I’m thinking about

Cross-region comparisons require care: differences in timing, reporting, and local context can affect how a policy appears to perform.
