Design Research Intern

Jacques Cousteau National Estuarine Research Reserve · Tuckerton, NJ

Tagged fish leave a trail of receiver data. My work at Jacques Cousteau NERR turned those records into an interactive ArcGIS map, letting researchers explore movement across locations and time.

Inside the work

Making movement visible

The ArcGIS map brought together time-enabled layers, species styling, and dynamic labels. These choices made it possible to follow movement over time and explore differences between species.

Keeping the map grounded in the research

Collaboration shaped both the data checks and its presentation. Accuracy mattered as much as the interface: the map needed to support conversations about species behavior and estuarine habitats.
